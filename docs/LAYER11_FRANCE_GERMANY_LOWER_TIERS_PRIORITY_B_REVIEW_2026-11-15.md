### Ligue 3 — important 2026/27 naming change

The FFF officially launched professional **Ligue 3** for 2026/27, replacing the former Championnat National. It is an 18-club single-poule competition with 34 home-and-away matchdays.

- FFF launch and format announcement: https://www.fff.fr/article/16778-la-fff-officialise-la-ligue-3-professionnelle.html
- Official 2026/27 calendar and 18-club participant list: https://www.fff.fr/article/17022-le-calendrier-2026-2027-est-servi.html
- Official Ligue 3 regulations: https://media.fff.fr/uploads/documents/textes-votes-a-l-ag-fff-du-06.06.2026.pdf

The name `Ligue 3` is valid for 2026/27. It must not be misclassified as Ligue 2 or continue to use the former National label as the new season's official name.
